# KPI Benchmarks

All values are **year-to-date vs the same period last year** (Jan 1 → latest business date, like-for-like).
Bands are calibrated from this model's own month-to-month volatility (Jan–Jun 2026 vs 2025).

| # | KPI | What it measures | Direction | 🟢 Green | 🟡 Amber | 🔴 Red | Severe |
|---|-----|------------------|-----------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 1 | Revenue Growth % | Revenue vs last year | Higher is better | ≥ +5% | 0% to +5% | < 0% | < −9% |
| 2 | Volume Growth % | Units sold vs last year | Higher is better | ≥ +3% | 0% to +3% | < 0% | < −11% |
| 3 | Footfall Growth % | Transaction count vs last year | Higher is better | ≥ +5% | 0% to +5% | < 0% | < −7% |
| 4 | ASP Growth % | Average selling price (rev ÷ units) | Higher is better | ≥ +1% | 0% to +1% | < 0% | — |
| 5 | ATV Growth % | Avg transaction value (rev ÷ bills) | Higher is better | ≥ 0% | −3% to 0% | < −3% | — |
| 6 | UPT Growth % | Units per transaction (units ÷ bills) | Higher is better | ≥ −2% | −5% to −2% | < −5% | — |
| 7 | Category Decline Breadth % | Share of categories declining | Lower is better | < 35% | 35% to 50% | > 50% | — |
| 8 | At-Risk Concentration Delta | Change in declining categories' revenue share | Lower is better | ≤ −6% | −6% to −4% | > −4% | — |
