# SB Mart Ageing insight rules

- Treat the report as one stock position as at the semantic model snapshot date.
- Use QAR and the model's Stock Value exposure only.
- Aged means over 9 months; high risk means over 12 months; oldest means over 24 months.
- Never add aged and non-moving totals because they overlap.
- Do not claim YoY, trend, causality, write-off amount, or recommended provision from one snapshot.
- Prioritize absolute QAR exposure before extreme percentages on immaterial members.
