What these figures cover, and what they do not

  • This is a comparison with the normal band, never with last year. Every figure is Wednesday 12 August 2026 placed against past days that match on the same weekday, so a Wednesday is only ever judged against other Wednesdays. The report holds no same-day-last-year figure and does not claim one.
  • The figures cover ST1 and ST4 only, and both stores are in every total on this page.
  • The bands are built from a small number of matching days. Net Sales, Bills and Basket Value use 25 matching past days; Margin uses 13 same-weekday readings. A band drawn from a short run of days is wider and moves more than one drawn from a long run.
  • A band at one level will not add up to the band at the level above it. The whole day finished SAR 98.79 below its floor while CONSUMER GOODS alone was SAR 1.4K below its own. Each level is worked out separately, so the two do not match and neither is wrong.
  • Bills cannot be added up. One basket holding bread, shampoo and a shirt is one Bill for the store but appears under each department, section and category it touched. Each figure on this page is taken from its own level, and department shares of baskets do not add to 100%.
  • Several groups share one name. A name such as LIFESTYLE or LADIES APPAREL covers more than one group in the source, and the source does not carry the code that separates them. Net Sales for those names is added, which is safe. Their bands are added too, which is close but is not a true benchmark for the whole name, and their Bills count some baskets more than once. Treat a band at these levels as a guide.
  • One department, OTHER, recorded no sale on this day and has been left out of the tables rather than shown as a zero.
  • The figures run to 12 Aug. That is the most recent day the report holds.

What these figures cover, and what they do not

  • This is a comparison with the normal band, never with last year. Every figure is Wednesday 12 August 2026 placed against past days that match on the same weekday, so a Wednesday is only ever judged against other Wednesdays. The report holds no same-day-last-year figure and does not claim one.
  • The figures cover ST1 and ST4 only, and both stores are in every total on this page.
  • The bands are built from a small number of matching days. Net Sales, Bills and Basket Value use 25 matching past days; Margin uses 13 same-weekday readings. A band drawn from a short run of days is wider and moves more than one drawn from a long run.
  • A band at one level will not add up to the band at the level above it. The whole day finished SAR 98.79 below its floor while CONSUMER GOODS alone was SAR 1.4K below its own. Each level is worked out separately, so the two do not match and neither is wrong.
  • Bills cannot be added up. One basket holding bread, shampoo and a shirt is one Bill for the store but appears under each department, section and category it touched. Each figure on this page is taken from its own level, and department shares of baskets do not add to 100%.
  • Several groups share one name. A name such as LIFESTYLE or LADIES APPAREL covers more than one group in the source, and the source does not carry the code that separates them. Net Sales for those names is added, which is safe. Their bands are added too, which is close but is not a true benchmark for the whole name, and their Bills count some baskets more than once. Treat a band at these levels as a guide.
  • One department, OTHER, recorded no sale on this day and has been left out of the tables rather than shown as a zero.
  • The figures run to 12 Aug. That is the most recent day the report holds.
