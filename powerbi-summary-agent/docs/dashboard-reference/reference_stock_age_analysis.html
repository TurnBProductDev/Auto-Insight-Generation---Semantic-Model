What these numbers do and do not say

  • Stock figures are a position as at 12 August 2026, not a total over a period.
  • Movement between age bands cannot be shown. The model holds only one stock position, so there is no previous snapshot to compare against. Showing how stock aged from one date to the next would need that history to be retained.
  • Aged means past the threshold for that division: six months for food, nine months for everything else. High-risk is a separate, universal overlay — anything over a year, in every division.
  • Aged stock and non-moving stock overlap by SAR 2.76M and are never added together.
  • Age resets when stock is transferred, so a store showing six-month-old stock means it arrived there six months ago — it may have been at a warehouse for longer before that.
  • This report says how old stock is and which of it is not selling. It does not say why stock is ageing, or what to do about it.
