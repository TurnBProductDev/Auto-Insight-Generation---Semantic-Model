How the score is worked out

Every SKU at every Location starts with 100 points, and six risks can deduct from it. Each risk is rebuilt from three dimensions: Value Impact (the risk value as a share of Stock Value, or of sales value for the two stockout risks), SKU Breadth (how many eligible SKUs carry it) and Duration or Severity (how long standing or how serious it is). They are weighted 50 / 30 / 20, or 70 / 30 for Damage which carries no Duration term. Each risk is capped at 25 points, so no single risk can cost more than a quarter of the score.

Four of the six risks compare a SKU against the 90th-percentile risk value of its peers in the same Category and Location, so a high-value Category cannot dominate simply because its prices are higher. Damage is already self normalised as a share of Stock Value and needs no benchmark.

A SKU that is already Out of Stock follows a deliberately harsher path. It starts from 0 rather than 100 and is not floored, so its score can go negative when other risks compound on top. 19,359 Loc-SKUs are on that path, averaging -5.5. They are labelled Out of Stock rather than placed in the five bands, because a stockout is not comparable with a stocked SKU’s risk profile.

The score is a summary, not a replacement for the numbers behind it. Every figure is in Overview, Locations, Divisions and Recommended Actions.

Things to know before using these numbers

  • The Inventory Health Score is calculated for the five Stores. Warehouse scoring is being added to the model. Until it is, the two Warehouses appear here as Stock Value and classification counts only, with no score, and are never added to the Store score.
  • This is a closing-stock snapshot as at 19 August 2026. It is a position, not a total over a period. Only Damage Value by month and the Stock Value trend cover a span of time.
  • A daily snapshot has only just started being retained. The line under “The story” shows the shape that chart will take, not real past scores.
  • The health-score model names one risk Dead Stock. The business term for that classification is Non-Moving, and this page uses Non-Moving throughout. They are the same thing. The model also names one risk Verge of Stockout, written here as On the Verge of Stockout.
  • Loc-SKUs and SKUs are different counts. A Loc-SKU is one SKU at one Location. 120,897 Loc-SKUs cover 44,504 distinct SKUs, so a SKU that is Non-Moving at three Stores appears three times in a Loc-SKU count and once in a SKU count. Each figure on this page states which it is.
  • One Section has no Excess threshold configured, so the Excess Stock rule cannot apply to its 5,583 Loc-SKUs holding USD 214,739. Its score of 64.1 is therefore flattering.
  • Opportunity Loss of USD 59,051 is an estimate based on average daily sales and retail price, not confirmed lost revenue. It covers Critical SKUs at Stores only, so it is much smaller than the unscoped stockout figure in the model. Never add it to Damage Value.
  • 7,896 Loc-SKUs holding USD 353,377 carry the Recommended Action NA or a missing reorder level, so the reorder decision cannot be assessed. They are shown as cannot be determined rather than counted as healthy.
  • Two model tables carry no relationship, so asking the model for a single Division’s score returns the company total. Every roll-up here is built from the main stock table, which reconciles to USD 4,964,930 exactly.
  • All money is stated in USD. Stock Value is at landing cost.

Things to know before using these numbers

  • Everything here is scoped to the 87,899 Loc-SKUs needing action. Totals will not match the all-stock view, and that is expected.
  • There is no Inventory Health Score on this view. The score is calculated across every scored Loc-SKU; recomputing it on the worst subset would not mean anything.
  • This is a closing-stock snapshot as at 19 August 2026, not a total over a period.
  • NON MOVING - ORDER PLACED returns no rows. BR-31 calls it one of the two double-warning states, so either nothing is in it or the source system is not producing it.
  • The five Recommended Action states left out cover the other 32,998 Loc-SKUs: 25,102 carrying STOCK AVAILABLE, NEW LISTED SKU or NOT ACTIVE, and 7,896 whose the reorder decision cannot be assessed.
