How the score is worked out

Each driver is measured against a business tolerance band — the target the business holds it to, and the floor at which it scores zero — then weighted. On today's figures the five weighted drivers add to 40. The bands and weights are printed on each card above so they can be argued with and changed.

The score is a summary, not a replacement for the numbers. Every figure behind it is in Locations, Divisions and Action queue.

What these numbers do and do not say

  • Stock figures are a position as at 12 August 2026, not a total over a period. The model keeps one snapshot, so no movement between dates can be shown.
  • Above cover is the surplus only — the value held beyond the agreed cover for that section and location — not the whole value of an overstocked product (SAR 21.13M above cover, on SAR 26.89M of overstocked lines).
  • Sales missed each day counts top-selling, locally bought products at stores only. Across every product and location the raw figure is SAR 293K a day, but that includes warehouses and imported products where it is not meaningful. It is an estimate from the recent rate of sale, not confirmed lost revenue.
  • FOOTWEAR has no cover threshold set, so SAR 2.03M of stock cannot be assessed for excess. Six sections are affected, holding SAR 2.09M in total.
  • NON MOVING - ORDER PLACED returned no lines. The rules list it as one of the two most urgent situations, so either nothing is in it, or the source system is not producing it.
