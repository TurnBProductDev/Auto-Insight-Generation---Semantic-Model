What these figures cover, and what they do not

  • This is a comparison with the normal band, never with last year. Every figure is Sunday 23 August 2026 placed against past days that match on the same weekday, so a Sunday is only ever judged against other Sundays. The report holds no same-day-last-year figure and does not claim one.
  • The figures cover ST1 and ST4 only, and both stores are in every total on this page.
  • The bands are built from a small number of matching days. Net Sales, Bills and Basket Value use 25 matching past days; Margin uses 13 same-weekday readings. A band drawn from a short run of days is wider and moves more than one drawn from a long run.
  • Net Sales arrives on two scales in the source, and has been put on one. Net Sales for each store and for the whole business is published on one scale in the source, while every benchmark band, every cost and every Net Sales figure below store level is published on another exactly 3.7037 times larger. Two independent measurements taken from this run's own rows agree on that figure, so the second set has been brought onto the first. Every level then adds up to the whole business. Department, section and category Net Sales each add up to the whole-business figure exactly, which is the check that this was done right.
  • A band at one level will not add up to the band at the level above it. The whole day finished USD 7.1K below its floor while CONSUMER GOODS alone was USD 3.6K below its own. Each level is worked out separately, so the two do not match and neither is wrong.
  • Bills cannot be added up. One basket holding bread, shampoo and a shirt is one Bill for the store but appears under each department, section and category it touched. Each figure on this page is taken from its own level, and department shares of baskets do not add to 100%.
  • Several groups share one name. A name at department, section or category level can cover more than one group in the source, and the source does not carry the code that separates them. Net Sales for those names is added, which is safe. Their bands are added too, which is close but is not a true benchmark for the whole name, and their Bills count some baskets more than once. Treat a band at these levels as a guide.
  • One department, OTHER, recorded no sale on this day and has been left out of the tables rather than shown as a zero.
  • The figures run to 23 Aug. That is the most recent day the report holds. Last updated 23 Aug 2026.
