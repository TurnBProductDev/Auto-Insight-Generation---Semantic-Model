# Key Insights
These year-on-year comparisons use the four like-for-like branches only: CFH014, CFH017, CFH018, and CFH021.

**CFH021 revenue increased by about SAR 4.3M and drove most of the group increase.** That was more than the total net increase because weaker results in other branches offset part of it. The gain was broad-based, led by Mens Fashion, Grocery Food, Electronics, and Personal Care, with both more transactions and more items sold contributing. Higher average revenue per item also supported the branch result, although that may reflect mix as well as price. Check whether these gains were sustained month by month or concentrated in one trading period.

**CFH022 added about SAR 11.9M of current sales, but this is new-branch revenue, not growth.** It also recorded about 2.1M units and 794K transactions with no last-year base, so it must stay outside all like-for-like percentages. Our checks showed this was a broad opening contribution rather than a single-category spike. Its reported total is also partial because the branch data stops on 13 June. Confirm whether the branch stopped trading then or whether the data feed stopped.

**Fresh Chicken and Parts revenue increased by about SAR 1.1M across the comparison group.** The increase was spread across all four branches, led by CFH014 and CFH018. More transactions and more items sold were the main contributors, while average revenue per item moved down slightly, which suggests the growth was demand-led rather than driven by higher item values. Check the monthly trend in CFH014 Fresh Chicken and CFH018 Fresh Chicken Parts.

**CFH017 revenue declined by about SAR 1.2M and was a major drag on the group result.** It also lost about 207K units and 86.6K transactions, so the weakness was not limited to pricing. The decline came mainly from lower sales in Fruit and Vegetables, Grocery Food, Mens Fashion, Roastery, and Personal Care, with fresh-food lines particularly important. That pattern is associated with weaker buying activity across several areas rather than one isolated issue. Review availability and in-stock position for the largest fresh-food declines in CFH017.

**Fruit and Vegetables revenue declined by about SAR 678K, mainly through lower volumes.** The biggest pressure came from Vegetables, Imported Fruits, Imported Vegetables, and Local Vegetables. The weakness was concentrated in CFH017, CFH021, and CFH018, while CFH014 partly offset it. Average revenue per item rose slightly, but that was not enough to overcome fewer items sold. Compare month-by-month performance for imported produce and vegetables by branch.

**Confectionery units declined by about 530K, with Chocolate Coated Bars driving most of the drop.** That was more than twice the total net unit decline for the comparison group because other categories grew. The drop was broad-based across all four branches, especially CFH021 and CFH018, and revenue fell much less than units while transactions slipped only slightly. That pattern suggests fewer items purchased per transaction were a likely contributor. Check the monthly unit trend for Chocolate Coated Bars by branch.

**March revenue declined by about SAR 2.0M, then April increased by about SAR 2.1M and nearly offset it.** The March shortfall was concentrated in Imported Fruits, Sun Flower Oil, and Imported Vegetables. Lower transactions and lower units were the main contributors, and all four branches were down in March. Because every branch moved the same way, calendar timing such as Ramadan or Eid may be relevant. Compare March and April against the trading calendar and promotion plan.

**Vegetable Oil revenue increased by about SAR 675K, led mainly by stronger sales in CFH014, CFH021, and CFH018.** Growth came from more transactions and more units sold across all four branches, with CFH014 the largest single contributor. Average revenue per item also moved up, which may reflect price and/or mix rather than volume alone. This made Vegetable Oil one of the clearest growth pockets in the comparison group. Review item-level mix or price changes, especially in CFH014.

**Fashion revenue increased by about SAR 739K, driven mainly by Mens Fashion.** The division grew on higher transactions and more units, with CFH021 contributing most of the increase. Average revenue per item moved down, so the gain appears to have come mainly from selling more items rather than from higher item values. That pattern suggests a shift toward faster-selling, lower-value lines within menswear. Review markdown and item mix changes in the main CFH021 menswear winners.

**Meat revenue increased by about SAR 742K, but lower average item value limited the gain.** More transactions and many more units sold lifted the category, mainly through Fresh Chicken and Fresh Chicken Parts. Those gains more than offset declines in Fresh Meat, especially beef and mutton lines. The pattern suggests the mix shifted toward chicken lines that sold more units at lower average item values. Compare item value and items per transaction within Meat, especially chicken versus beef and mutton.

**Sun Flower Oil revenue declined by about SAR 559K across all four branches.** CFH014 and CFH021 were the largest drags, but every branch declined. Fewer transactions and fewer units sold were the main contributors, while higher average revenue per item only partly softened the decline. This looks more like lost buying activity than a simple pricing issue. Check the monthly pattern in CFH014 and CFH021 for any promotion, availability, or calendar timing effect.

**FMCG FOOD revenue increased by about SAR 714K, but units declined by about 365K.** The division remained a large part of the business, and the unit decline was broad-based across all four branches. Chocolate was the largest named unit drag, while oils and other lines appear to have supported revenue growth. That suggests higher average revenue per item and mix were likely contributors rather than stronger volume. Review a full division split of units versus average item value to confirm what sustained the revenue gain.

# Data Quality Watch-outs
- CFH022 has no prior-year values, so any growth percentage that includes it would be misleading. Our checks showed SAR 11.9M of current revenue, 2.1M units, and 794K transactions with zero last-year base; verify that all like-for-like reporting continues to exclude CFH022 from percentages and rankings.
- CFH022 data stops on 13 June, so its current total is not a full-period branch result. Our checks showed the branch total is partial by coverage date; verify whether this is a branch closure or a broken data feed before using the figure operationally.
- Several category and product breakdowns came from top-N slices rather than full distributions. Our checks still identified clear leading contributors, but verify full non-truncated cuts before using them for exhaustive ranking or exact shares by subcategory.
- The final month in the current period is incomplete. Verify whether the stored last-year values cover the same part-month before treating any latest-month comparison as fully aligned.

- CFH022 new-branch contribution: high confidence. Supported by trusted entity-scope results and explicit business rules. Main caveat is the branch data stops on 13 June, so the total is partial-period.
- Fresh Chicken and Parts growth: high confidence on the size and broad branch spread, supported by reconciled decompositions and accepted investigation summary. Caveat: no further query drill was run in this trail, so operational explanations remain outside the model.

- Fruit and Vegetables decline: medium-high confidence. Core size and branch pattern are solid from the accepted investigation and reconciled decomposition, but some subgroup details came from partial ranked slices rather than full distributions.
- Confectionery volume decline: medium-high confidence. The category and branch concentration are clearly stated in the accepted investigation, but no additional query rows were included in this trail.
- March versus April shift: high confidence on the existence and breadth of the swing. Supported by complete month coverage and three successful March drills. Caveat: the model cannot prove whether the shift reflects Ramadan/Eid timing, promotions, or availability.
- Vegetable Oil growth: high confidence. Supported by reconciled decomposition and a successful full branch drill for the segment. Caveat: the model cannot isolate item-level price versus mix inside the bucket.
- Fashion growth: medium confidence. Division-level growth and decomposition are solid, but many detailed menswear winners were stated only in the accepted conclusion and are not backed by query rows in the supplied trail.
- Meat growth with lower unit value: medium-high confidence. The division-level split is strong and reconciled, and the chicken-versus-fresh-meat pattern is consistent with other supplied signals. Caveat: no new query rows were included in this trail.
- Sun Flower Oil decline: high confidence. Supported by reconciled decomposition and a successful full branch drill. Caveat: the model has no deeper explanatory split below this named bucket.
- FMCG FOOD revenue up but units down: medium confidence. The broad pattern is clear, but the investigation explicitly says the available product-group slice is partial and does not allow a full division decomposition.
- General limitations:
  - Several breakdowns in `MIS_DEEP_DIVE2` are marked partial and not fully trusted in the coverage matrix.

  - Sales and units are monthly only; there is no daily sales comparison available.
