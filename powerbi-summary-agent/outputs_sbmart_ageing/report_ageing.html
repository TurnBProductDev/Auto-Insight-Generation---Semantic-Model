What stands out

QAR 91K of stock is more than two years old, 1.5% of all stock value as at 2026-08-19. This is the most likely write-off candidate and is reported separately from the 12-24 month band.

A further QAR 370K is between one and two years old (6.0% of stock value). Together with the 24+ month band, QAR 461K is classed as high-risk, 7.4% of all stock.

QAR 285K is both aged and non-moving - old stock with no recorded sales. This is the highest-risk combination. It is not added to the aged total, because the two measures overlap.

QAR 372K of stock under nine months old has recorded no sales since it arrived. That is a demand or placement signal rather than an age problem.

Aged stock totals QAR 825K, 13.3% of the QAR 6.20M held across all locations. The configured ageing policy defines the threshold used for this report.

What this report does not show

Stock age movement between two dates cannot be shown, because the model holds only one stock position (a single snapshot date). Showing it would need the previous snapshot to be retained.

The semantic model's [aged stock share] was excluded: its numerator does not reconcile to the Stock Value exposure used by this report.

This report shows how old stock is and which aged stock is not selling. It does not say why stock is ageing, or what to do about it.
